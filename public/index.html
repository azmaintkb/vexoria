Where do you deliver?+

Delivery is free for customers from North South University (NSU), American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB), and Independent University, Bangladesh (IUB). Other deliveries cost Tk 70 inside Dhaka and Tk 130 outside Dhaka. Choose your delivery location at checkout. Delivery takes up to 48 hours.

How do I pay?+

Pay in cash when your order arrives. You don’t need an account or an advance payment.

How do I place an order?+

Choose an available product, add it to your bag, and enter your name, Bangladesh mobile number, and delivery address. You can also use our social ordering options when available.

Can I return my order?+

We do not accept returns. Please review the product details and your order carefully before confirming.

How should I care for my thermal pot?+

Check the care instructions on the product listing or packaging. Product-specific materials, capacity, and other details will be included when each item is published.
